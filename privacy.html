Privacy Policy

How the Pawtrait preview handles your photos and form details.

Last updated: 18 September 2026 · Preview-stage information

About this notice

This notice describes the current Pawtrait preview website. The store is not accepting orders or receiving enquiry forms yet. Operator identification, a privacy contact and the full commercial data-handling details will be published before those services open.

Your photos and design details

Selecting a photo displays it locally in your browser. The website does not send the photo, pet name, design preferences or contact-form entries to a Pawtrait server. It does not save them to local storage or a customer database.

A downloaded design brief contains the text you entered and the photo filename, not the photo itself. A downloaded enquiry contains the contact details and message you entered. These files remain on your device unless you choose to share them.

Keeping and deleting your drafts

Close or refresh the page to end the current preview session. Your browser may retain form entries through its own autofill or session-restoration features. Delete downloaded files from your device when you no longer need them. Pawtrait cannot delete files stored on your device.

Cookies & external services

Pawtrait’s own page code does not set cookies or use advertising pixels, analytics scripts or browser storage. The website’s hosting and access services may process connection information such as your IP address, browser details and access logs.

Fonts load from Google Fonts. The map on the Contact page loads from OpenStreetMap. Loading these resources makes requests to those providers, which may receive your IP address and browser information. Facebook and Pinterest receive information when you follow their sharing links. Their privacy policies apply to their services.

Google privacy information · OpenStreetMap privacy information · Hosting platform privacy information

Before orders and enquiries open

This notice will be updated to identify the business and privacy contact and explain the purposes, legal bases, recipients, retention periods, any international transfers and applicable rights for customer data. A route for privacy requests must be available before direct collection begins.

The contact form currently saves an unsent draft only; do not use it to submit a privacy request. Details still needed for a complete operational notice are described in the ICO’s privacy-information guidance.
