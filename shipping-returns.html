Shipping & Returns

Clear expectations for made-to-order pieces, before you buy.

Last updated: 18 September 2026 · Preview-stage information

Ordering status

The collection is currently a preview. Checkout is not open and no products are being ordered through this site. Downloading a design brief does not reserve an item or start production.

Production and delivery

The planned service uses production partners to make personalised products to order and ship them directly. Confirmed production locations, shipping destinations, costs, dispatch times and delivery estimates will be shown before purchase. No guaranteed delivery date is offered in this preview.

Check before production

Before a real order is placed, confirm the product, measurements, colour, pet name, message and design. The final design-review process and the point after which changes are no longer possible will be explained before payment.

Changes, returns and problems

The operational cancellation, return, refund and faulty-item procedures have not yet been set. They will be published with a working support contact before orders open. This page does not impose a blanket “no returns” rule or limit applicable consumer rights.

Planning a special gift?

You can prepare your product idea and preferred date in a custom request draft. It stays on your device and is not submitted. Please wait for a confirmed quote and delivery estimate before relying on a gift deadline.
