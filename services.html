A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good questions.

What kind of photo should I use?

A sharp, well-lit photo with your pet’s face fully visible works best. Avoid heavy filters, screenshots and cropped ears. The preview accepts JPG, PNG and WebP files up to 10 MB.

Can I include more than one pet?

Tell us how many pets you’d like in your design. Feasibility and pricing will depend on the product and layout, and will need to be confirmed before ordering.

Can I add a name or a memorial message?

Yes. Include the exact spelling and wording in your design brief. We can plan a joyful everyday piece or a thoughtful memorial keepsake.

How much will it cost, and when will it arrive?

Final prices and delivery estimates are not published yet. They will depend on the product, customisation and destination. No payment is taken on this preview website.

What about sizing, changes and returns?

Size guides, the design approval process and the applicable returns policy will be available before checkout opens. Personalised orders need careful checking; please confirm names, sizes and design details before production.

Where does my uploaded photo go?

In this preview, your photo is displayed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to a server or included in the downloaded text brief. You will need to supply the original photo when a real order channel is available.
