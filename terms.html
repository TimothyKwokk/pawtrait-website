What you can do on the Pawtrait preview, and what happens before ordering opens.

Last updated: 18 September 2026 · Preview-stage information

Using this preview

You can browse the collection, preview your photo and download a design brief or enquiry draft. No online purchase, production request or contract of sale is created by using these features. No payment is collected.

Products, images and pricing

Collection photos show product-style inspiration rather than completed Pawtrait customer orders. QQ’s photos show our first pet model. Final materials, garment measurements, print methods, colour options, prices, taxes and shipping charges are not confirmed by the preview.

Photos and personalisation

Use photos and text that you own or have permission to use. Check spellings and design preferences carefully. Previewing a photo does not give Pawtrait permission to use it in marketing, and the current website does not receive that photo.

Before placing an order

Business identity and contact details, product specifications, the approval process, total price, payment arrangements, fulfilment estimates and the applicable cancellation and returns terms will need to be available before checkout opens.

See Shipping & Returns for the current launch status. Nothing on this page is intended to remove any applicable statutory consumer rights.

External links and changes

Links to maps, social networks and other third-party websites lead to services with their own terms and policies. We may update this preview and will date revisions to these terms. Contact details are still being prepared; the current enquiry form does not send a message.
