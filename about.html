OUR STORY

A small idea.
A very familiar feeling.

Pawtrait began with a simple idea: the photos we cherish shouldn’t have to stay on our phones.

A sleepy expression. A muddy adventure. A look that says everything. These are the details that make a pet part of the family. We’re building a small independent brand around bringing them into your everyday life.

From a personal T-shirt to a gift for someone who misses their companion, each piece begins with a photo and a connection.
